Local conditions matter

Landscaping made for hills, rain, shade, and real life.

Pittsburgh properties can be beautiful and complicated. A strong plan considers how water moves, where sunlight lands, how you use the yard, and what will still look good next season.

  • Right plant, right place.
    Plant choices matched to light, soil, exposure, and upkeep.
  • Usable outdoor space.
    Patios, paths, and beds planned around how people actually move.
  • Practical property solutions.
    Thoughtful grading, drainage direction, edges, and retaining elements.
Our Approach

A simpler project

From “what should we do?” to a clear next step.

Good landscape work starts before anything is planted or built. The process keeps the plan, scope, and expectations easy to understand.

01

Tell us about the space

Share the property, goals, photos, priorities, and the rough timing you have in mind.

02

Walk the property

Review access, grade, drainage, light, existing materials, and how you want to use the area.

03

Approve the plan

Receive a defined scope with recommended work, expected sequence, and clear pricing.

04

Bring it to life

Schedule the work, stay informed, and see the outdoor space take shape.
