The Rivers & Roots approach

Start with the property. Design for the people who live there.

Every yard has a set of conditions: light, soil, grade, drainage, access, architecture, and the time someone wants to spend maintaining it. Good landscaping works with those realities.

That is why the first conversation focuses on how the outdoor space should function—not just a list of plants or products. A front entry should feel welcoming. A patio should fit the way people gather. A retaining solution should make the property more useful. A maintenance plan should be realistic.

The result is a clearer project and a finished space that feels connected to the home.

Pittsburgh is part of the plan.

Hills, shade, rain, clay soil, older homes, and distinct seasons all influence the right recommendation.
